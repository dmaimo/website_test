About

My research examines how political institutions shape economic outcomes, from political competition and growth, freedom and redistribution, and bargaining in conflict resolution to the spatial dimension of regional development. I teach graduate courses in international public policy, financial econometrics and public sector economics, and I direct the PhD Programme in Economics, Management and Statistics.

I graduated in Law at the University of Messina and then moved to the United Kingdom, where I earned an M.Sc. in Economics at the University of York and a Ph.D. in Economics at the University of Essex under the supervision of Prof. Abhinay Muthoo. In parallel, I completed a Doctorate in Economics and Institutions at the University of Messina, supervised by Prof. Pietro Navarra. My full CV is available here.
