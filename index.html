About

After graduating in Law at the University of Messina, I moved to the United Kingdom, where I was awarded an M.Sc. in Economics from the University of York and a Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Essex, under the supervision of Prof. Abhinay Muthoo. In the meantime, I obtained a Doctorate in Economics and Institutions from the University of Messina under the supervision of Prof. Pietro Navarra.

My research studies how political institutions shape economic outcomes: political competition and growth, freedom and redistribution, bargaining in conflict resolution, and the spatial dimension of regional development. I teach graduate courses in international public policy, financial econometrics and geopolitical economics. You can download my full cv here
