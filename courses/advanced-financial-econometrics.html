Advanced Financial Econometrics

Graduate EN A.Y. 2025–26

Econometric methods for financial time series and asset pricing: volatility modelling, realized measures from high-frequency data, and applications using Stata and related tools. The module is taught in the two-year M.Sc. in Financial Markets. Slides, datasets and Stata do-files used during the lectures can be downloaded below; additional material will be posted on this page.
