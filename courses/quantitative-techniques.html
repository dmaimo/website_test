Quantitative Techniques

PhD EN

Introduction to Stata, an econometric package useful to carry out empirical analysis. The module will cover OLS estimator, test of hypotheses, violation of assumptions of CLRM, static (fixed and random effects) and dynamic (Arellano and Bond; Blundell and Bond; bias-corrected estimators) panel data estimations. Please refer to the library website to have information about how to install Stata in your machine.
