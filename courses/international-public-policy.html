International Public Policy

Graduate EN

An analysis of how political institutions — democratic and non-democratic, competitive and non-competitive — shape public policy choices and economic outcomes, with applications to growth, redistribution and international political economy. The course is taught in the M.Sc. in International Management (LM-77), Department of Economics, University of Messina.
